BrandOrbit — Brand Playbook Excerpt

Packaging identity: Matte finish only, no gloss lamination — this is a
non-negotiable brand standard across all SKUs, agreed by founders in Q1 2026.
Primary palette: Terracotta, cream, deep forest green.
Tone of voice: Warm, direct, no corporate jargon. Avoid exclamation marks
in packaging copy.
Launch cadence: New SKU launches target the first week of each quarter to
align with influencer seeding timelines.
Return policy standard: 30-day no-questions-asked returns, stated on every
product page and packaging insert.
